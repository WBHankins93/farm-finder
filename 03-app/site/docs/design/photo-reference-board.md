# FarmFinder photo reference board

> First-pass art-direction references for future original image generation.
> Curated and link-verified on 2026-08-27. No image on this page is a licensed
> FarmFinder production asset.

## Use policy

These Shutterstock records are **thematic references only**. The links point to
watermarked previews or asset pages; no preview files are stored in this
repository. Do not publish, download, edit, trace, or present these references
as FarmFinder imagery without a separate licensing decision.

For future GPT image generation, combine general traits from several references:
camera distance, crop, natural-light behavior, material texture, seasonal color,
and the amount of visible work. Do not attach a Shutterstock image as an edit
input or ask a model to recreate its distinctive composition, person, property,
signage, or location. Generated people and places must be fictional and must not
imply that they are directory members.

Generation progress, regional specialization, and the full accuracy review gate
live in the [regional imagery production plan](regional-imagery-production-plan.md).
Generated preview files remain local and outside this repository.

### Collection lens

- **Favor:** independent-farm scale, working environments, imperfect produce,
  restrained color, natural light, soil and weather texture, candid activity,
  diverse ages and backgrounds, and useful landscape plus portrait crops.
- **Reject:** staged smiles, spotless faux-rustic sets, industrial scale,
  excessive saturation, prominent brands, readable private details, generic
  agribusiness symbolism, or scenery that misrepresents a real location.
- **Geography:** FarmFinder is nationwide. Louisiana-specific references are
  labeled explicitly; `LA` always means Louisiana, never Los Angeles. Regional
  imagery should add specificity without making the product Gulf-South-only.

## Synthesized generation briefs

These briefs deliberately combine traits from multiple records. Use them as the
starting point for a later generation pass, then vary subject, season, geography,
and crop rather than treating them as fixed templates.

### Brief A — The honest harvest

Draw from FF-REF-001 through FF-REF-004: tactile produce, irregular abundance,
one clear direction of daylight, and useful negative space. Generate both a 3:2
landscape and 4:5 portrait as separate original scenes. Keep color inside the
FarmFinder paper/green/rust family, but preserve natural food color. Avoid perfect
flat lays, excessive props, mist, gloss, labels, and artificial “farmhouse” decor.

### Brief B — The farm is a workplace

Draw from FF-REF-005 through FF-REF-008: diversified rows, affordable protective
structures, diffused greenhouse light, and people absorbed in real tasks. Show
maintenance and infrastructure without romanticizing hardship. Prefer eye-level
or field-level views, modest acreage, and ordinary tools. Avoid drones, enormous
monocultures, spotless glasshouses, heroic poses, and decorative red barns.

### Brief C — Food carried by hands

Draw from FF-REF-009 through FF-REF-012: close physical scale, soil, workwear,
shallow focus, and crops still connected to their growing environment. Build
fictional hands and bodies with varied ages and skin tones across the set. Avoid
copying gestures, manicured advertising hands, dramatic dirt smearing, anonymous
labor stereotypes, or faces used without narrative reason.

### Brief D — Buying is a conversation

Draw from FF-REF-013 through FF-REF-016: eye-lines, mid-gesture exchanges, pulp
cartons, modest boxes, and believable next steps such as pickup or delivery.
Photograph the interaction candidly with products still readable. Avoid posed
checkout scenes, forced cheer, crowded festival spectacle, branded bags, readable
private addresses, or signs promising availability the directory cannot verify.

### Brief E — Beyond vegetables

Draw from FF-REF-017 through FF-REF-020: pasture behavior, collected eggs,
hands-on apiary care, and simple value-added product portraits. Keep animal welfare
and husbandry visible without making unsupported certification claims. Avoid
isolated animal collages, factory interiors, costume-like rural styling, pristine
packaging, fake labels, and sentimental holiday imagery.

### Brief F — A nationwide atlas with regional truth

Draw from FF-REF-021 through FF-REF-024: Louisiana Gulf seafood, Louisiana rice,
citrus orchard depth, and compact urban production. Create each as a separate,
geographically plausible scene, with location labels added only in editorial
metadata—not inside the image. Avoid blending incompatible crops and coastlines
into one fantasy farm, avoid using `LA` to mean Los Angeles, and balance the final
library with future references from other U.S. regions.
