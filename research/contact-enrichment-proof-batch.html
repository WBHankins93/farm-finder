The canonical database (research/local_farm_database_final.xlsx, "All Farms" tab, 315 farms) has 148 farms with no phone number — 83 in Louisiana, 65 in Mississippi. Rather than blind-research all 148 (slow, and many small farms simply aren't findable online), I ran a proof batch of 18: Louisiana farms with no phone but an active market presence — the exact profile your outreach plan cares about. Most are Crescent City Farmers Market (CCFM) vendors, which turned out to matter.

Headline finding. A direct phone is only findable about a third of the time — but nearly every farm has a findable market schedule and/or website/email. For outreach and for the future app, the higher-value enrichment isn't the phone at all: it's the where-to-find-them schedule plus a web/email contact. The phone gap is real, but it's the wrong single metric to chase.

What the batch proves

  • Phones are the hardest field, ~38% findable. Small direct-to-consumer farms deliberately route contact through Facebook/Instagram DM or a website form. Chasing "phone for all 148" would be low-yield and slow.
  • Schedules and web/email are ~88% findable and are more useful anyway — a farm's market days are what an app user (and an outreach call) actually needs. CCFM/Market Umbrella vendor pages are a goldmine: one source yields schedule + products + often contact for dozens of NOLA-metro farms at once.
  • The database has accuracy debt. This batch alone surfaced 8 corrections — several farms tagged "no website" have one (Cicada, Nola Tilth, Know Dat Grow Dat), and two are mis-located (Mycelium = Pearl River, Micro Pharms = Baton Rouge). Enrichment is also a data-QA pass, not just a fill-in-the-blanks.
Recommendation before you spend effort on all 148.
Reframe the task from "find missing phones" to "enrich market schedule + best contact channel," and mine the aggregator sources first (CCFM / Market Umbrella vendor directory, Grow Acadiana, EatWild, Black Farmers Index) rather than searching farm-by-farm. Rough plan:
  • Tier 1 — CCFM / market-roster farms (~40–60 of the 148): high yield, batchable from a handful of directory pages. Best ROI; do these next.
  • Tier 2 — farms with a website or Square store but no phone: scrape the contact page. Medium yield.
  • Tier 3 — social-only / no digital footprint: flag as "phone requires field/manual outreach." Don't burn research time here; these get a phone when Sproutflow actually calls.
Net: expect to enrich schedule/contact for well over half the 148, but accept that a stubborn tail (~20–30%) will only get a phone through direct outreach — which is fine, because that's the outreach step anyway.
