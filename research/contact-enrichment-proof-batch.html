What this proves

  • Direct phones remain the hardest field, ~40% findable per farm — the batch and the DB pass agree on this. Chasing "a phone for every farm" is low-yield; the DB's own Contact Status split (244 direct / 41 social-only / 14 none) is the honest picture.
  • An independent pass still finds gaps. Even after a QA sweep, a second set of eyes surfaced 4 missing phones and a location error (Micro Pharms = Baton Rouge, not New Orleans; Mycelium = Pearl River). Enrichment benefits from a reconciliation step, not just a single pass.
  • Schedule + channel > phone. For the app and for outreach, a farm's market days and best contact channel are more useful and far more findable (~88%) than a phone. The DB's new Contact Status column already encodes this insight — good.
Recommendation — the task has evolved; adjust accordingly.
  • Don't run a separate manual sweep. A real pipeline now exists (verification columns + QA queue). Feed the 4 found phones into it and move on.
  • Target the 55, not the 73. The 244 "direct contact" farms are done. Focus remaining effort on the 41 social-only + 14 no-contact — these need Instagram/Facebook DM or field/manual outreach, not more web search. That's genuinely the Sproutflow outreach step, so don't burn research budget pretending otherwise.
  • Add a schedule + channel field, not just a phone field. Structure Market Schedule (day/time/location) and keep Contact Status as the primary "reachability" metric.
